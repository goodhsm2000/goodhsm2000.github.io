- I am a **Research Intern** at the **Autonomous Robot Intelligence Laboratory (ARIL)**, **Seoul National University**

- My research focuses on **language-model-augmented robot navigation** to improve autonomy in challenging real-world conditions.  

- My goal is to **overcome the limitations and failure modes of autonomous driving systems**—especially in complex, dynamic, and long-tail scenarios—by enabling robots to make **more reliable, context-aware decisions** and achieve **robust navigation performance**.
